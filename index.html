Interseptor v2.16.0 is out

Catch every request before it lands.

Interseptor is an intercepting proxy and pentest workbench in one Go binary. Hold live traffic, change it, replay it and write up the finding, by hand or with your AI agent.

Intercept

Waiting

Tap a floating request and Kirby inhales it. You can change it before it reaches the server.

One binary. The whole workbench.

Everything a web pentest needs between the browser and the server, without a JVM to warm up, a license key, or a cap on how many findings you can export.

What you get

Hold requests and responses mid-flight, edit them, then forward or drop. Match-and-replace rules take care of edits you make every time.

Install with Go 1.25 or later

go install github.com/Veyal/interseptor/cmd/interseptor@latest

Or hand it to your AI agent

Read https://veyal.github.io/interseptor/llms-full.txt and follow it.

For systems you own or have written permission to test.
