detector · how it works

The ECB tell

ECB runs every 16-byte block through AES on its own. Two equal plaintext blocks give two equal ciphertext blocks, so patterns in the message survive encryption. CBC, CFB, OFB and CTR mix in an IV or counter, so equal blocks come out different.

What it cannot tell

No repeats does not prove CBC: ECB over text that never repeats looks random too. CTR, CFB, OFB and GCM cannot be told apart from the bytes alone, so supply the key to test them. Key sizes 128, 192 and 256 bits are all tried.
